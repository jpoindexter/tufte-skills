# Notice on Scope and Copyright

## What the MIT license covers

The [MIT license](LICENSE) covers the skill documents, the installer, and the
surrounding code in this repository — the original reference prose written for
this project.

## What it does not cover

It does not and cannot cover Edward Tufte's published work.

The skills in this repository are **original documents** that explain Tufte's
principles in their own words. Direct quotations are brief, single-sentence,
and attributed, used for educational commentary rather than reproduction. No
skill reproduces a substantial portion of any copyrighted work. Those
quotations remain the property of their copyright holders.

## Sources

The five source books are published by Graphics Press and must be purchased
separately:

1. *The Visual Display of Quantitative Information* (1983; 2nd ed. 2001)
2. *Envisioning Information* (1990)
3. *Visual Explanations* (1997)
4. *Beautiful Evidence* (2006)
5. *Seeing With Fresh Eyes* (2020)

Buy them at [edwardtufte.com/books](https://www.edwardtufte.com/books/). They
are the source, and they are better than any summary of them.
